1. Introduction

Designed to Breakthrough LLC (“DTB,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and safeguard personal information from clients, website visitors and partners in accordance with applicable Pennsylvania and federal law.

2. Information we collect

  • Personal information: name, email address, phone number, business name and billing details provided during consultations, contracts or form submissions.
  • Project information: brand assets, digital files, credentials and materials supplied for service execution.
  • Technical data: usage metrics, browser type, IP address and analytics data used for security and performance.
  • Payment information: payment confirmations may be processed by third-party providers and are not stored on DTB’s website servers.

3. How we use information

  • Providing contracted services and managing projects.
  • Communicating about project updates, invoices and support.
  • Marketing and portfolio representation consistent with the applicable client agreement.
  • Security, analytics and service improvement.

DTB does not sell, lease or trade personal data.

4. Data retention

DTB retains information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations and maintain operational records.

5. Information sharing

Limited information may be shared with authorized team members or contractors under confidentiality obligations, vendors used for service delivery, or legal authorities when required by law or necessary to protect DTB’s rights and property.

6. Data security

DTB uses reasonable administrative, technical and access controls intended to protect information. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your rights

You may request access to information DTB holds about you, request correction or deletion where applicable, or withdraw consent for marketing communications. Send requests to inquire@dtbsolutions.tech.

8. Portfolio and marketing use

Use of completed work in DTB’s portfolio or marketing is governed by the applicable proposal, client services agreement and any separate confidentiality terms.

9. Changes to this policy

DTB may update this policy periodically. The effective date shown above identifies the current published version.

10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries or data requests, email inquire@dtbsolutions.tech.
